Safe Harbor

I couldn’t stay mad at you
even if you loaded the dishwasher wrong
even if you didn’t listen when I was right
even when you overreact to me losing track of time, forgetting to reply because of the before era
before the million things we hadn’t done alone, on the road, I walked between abandoned warehouses. At intervals, I scanned
my surroundings, checked Google maps for your dot
double checked I’m walking the right way. I avoided
walking too close to doorways. You parked the Prius near the streetlight. I saw you look up from the iPad when I was in the last half block.
I read the worry in your eyebrows when you watched me shiver.
First love must have told you. He said you’d get here faster. I closed the car door
and breathed out
jaggedly. You tucked the iPad into the laptop bag. Do you want a hug?, you asked softly. You waited
until I managed
a nod. Hugged me
like you were afraid
of breaking me. We clicked our seatbelts.
Took a breath.
Drove away.
