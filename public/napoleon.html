I wake up believing I’m Napoleon

I wake up believing I’m Napoleon,
postulates M from the back seat,
I’m far more likely in an asylum than
being actual Napoleon. I glance back at you from shotgun
with a wry smile through the visor mirror.
The driver doesn’t notice. You suppress a chuckle, shake your head
look down, then off to the side,
scratch your head absentmindedly. What?, demands M. I raise my left hand to start to reply.
I glance at you through my fingers, catch
your eyes flicker, feel the ‘no’ in my chest. I blink-nod,
summon a theatrical smile. Because you’re saying there are far more
instances of the former than the latter?,
I ask M as though for clarification.
